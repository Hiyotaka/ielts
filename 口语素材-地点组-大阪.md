# 口语素材 — 地点组：大阪

## Part 2: Favorite City You Visited（核心回答）

I'd like to talk about Osaka, hands down my favorite city. It's in the Kansai region of Japan — famous for street food, shopping districts, and a really vibrant vibe.

I first got curious about Osaka through those street food walking tours on Bilibili. After watching a bunch, I really wanted to see what daily life in a Japanese city actually feels like.

I went there in June 2025, right after graduation — kind of a celebration trip. A one-week thing with a close friend. We stayed near Shinsaibashi, super convenient, right in the middle of everything. We also hopped over to Kyoto, then took a night bus to Tokyo, but honestly, Osaka was the highlight.

What really struck me was how orderly everything was. The streets are spotless — yet there are almost no trash bins anywhere. The subway is so punctual it's almost funny. But it never felt dull. Walk around Shinsaibashi at night and it's just buzzing — neon signs everywhere, people lining up for takoyaki.

The most memorable spot was the Umeda Sky Building. I went up to the floating observatory — you're standing between two towers — and the night view was breathtaking. The whole city spread out beneath us, this endless grid of lights stretching to the horizon. That was the moment I realized Osaka was way bigger than I'd pictured.

So yeah, looking back, Osaka stuck with me not just because it was impressive — but because of what it meant at that time. It felt like a reward for four years of university. Like closing one chapter and starting another.
